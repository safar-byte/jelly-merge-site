Delete my data

Jelly Merge creates an anonymous player (a random id, a name and your scores) so you can appear on the leaderboard. You can delete it any time.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

This deletes your player, your scores, your name and the link to Google Play Games / Game Center from our server, and your progress on the device. It cannot be undone. Saved games kept by Google Play Games or iCloud are controlled by those services (Play Games settings > Delete data; iPhone Settings > Apple ID > iCloud).

If you no longer have the game

Send an e-mail from the address you use to contact us to safarmuhmmed@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Jelly Merge data" and, if you remember it, your name on the board. If you cannot prove which player is yours we may only be able to delete data that you can identify (for example the name and a high score). We answer within 30 days.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Anonymous audit entries (score checks) older than 30 days are removed automatically. Purchase records stay with Google or Apple under their rules.
