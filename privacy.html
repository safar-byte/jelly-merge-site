Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026. Applies to the game Jelly Merge (package com.safarbyte.jellymerge) by Safar Byte ("we").

Short version

We do not ask for your e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos or location. The game stores your progress on your device, puts a random player id, a name and your scores on a public leaderboard, and shows ads provided by Google AdMob.

Ads and your choices

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions you are asked for consent first and you can change it any time in Settings > Privacy options (ads). You can also reset or limit your advertising id in your phone settings, or buy "Remove ads" (optional reward ads, for example to revive, always stay optional). See how Google uses data.

Who receives the data

Supabase (hosting of the leaderboard database), Google (ads, Play Games, Play Billing) and Apple (Game Center, App Store) as described above. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for anything other than running the game, keeping the leaderboard fair and showing ads.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. The database cannot be read directly: the app can only call a few functions that check your secret key. Names are visible to all players, so please do not use your real name.

Your rights, deletion and retention

You can delete everything we hold about you in the game: Settings > Delete my data. This erases your player, scores, store link and name from our server and the progress on your device. If you no longer have the game, see how to request deletion. Data of players that were never active is removed automatically after 30 days. Under the GDPR and similar laws you may also ask for access, correction or deletion by e-mail, and you may complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect their data; write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when something changes.
